**ROOT CAUSE, MEASURED** (simulator, camera pinned from code at pitch 57 = the head unit's chase pitch, zoom 16.5,
12 callouts at 4 ground distances, 3 mechanisms side by side): Mapbox multiplies symbol `icon-size`/`text-size` by a
perspective ratio `0.5 + 0.5*(D/w)`, so a billboarded callout SHRINKS with distance; `text-translate` is a paint
property in constant screen POINTS and is not scaled by it, so the text climbed out of the shrinking box.
Text centre below the box top, box width 69.7 → 59.3 pt across +350 m: **translate 16.5 → 10.0**, **ems 16.5 → 13.7**.
Error is `25 × (1 − ratio)` pt: 1.8 at 300 m, 3.6 at 500 m, 6.2 at 1 km. **At pitch 0 all three are identical**, which
is why every flat check passed. Fix: `text-offset` in EMS, which rides `text-size`. `src/calloutTextOffset.ts`
(the measurement is in the file), gate `tools/sim-qc/callout_offset_test.mts` (19 assertions incl. 4 negative
controls), trap-check rule `callout-text-translate-in-points` (14 rules). Memory: `mapbox-perspective-ratio-vs-paint-translate`.
